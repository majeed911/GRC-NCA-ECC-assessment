# Remediation Plan — TechNova Solutions

Corrective actions for each risk identified in the [Risk Register](./risk-register.md), prioritized by risk severity.

---

## Critical Priority

| Risk ID | Remediation Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all accounts, with no exceptions | IT team, under IT Manager oversight | 1 week |
| R-03 | Establish a formal risk management process (identification, assessment, treatment, monitoring) | IT Manager, in coordination with senior management (interim owner, pending a dedicated GRC/security hire) | 20 working days |
| R-05 | Document and formally approve cybersecurity roles and responsibilities for each relevant department/position | IT Manager, in collaboration with HR (for formal sign-off and record-keeping) | 15 working days |
| R-07 | Fully update the cybersecurity strategy to reflect current threats and NCA ECC requirements, with a recurring (at least annual) review cycle | IT Manager, in coordination with senior management (interim, pending a dedicated security manager) | 4–6 weeks |
| R-10 | Immediately deactivate the two open former-employee accounts; integrate the offboarding process with HR systems so an employee's last working day automatically triggers same-day account deactivation | IT (execution) + HR (notification/coordination) | 1 day for immediate deactivation; 2 weeks for the automated process |

## High Priority

| Risk ID | Remediation Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-02 | Deploy and enforce a mandatory enterprise VPN for all remote access to internal systems | IT team | 3–4 weeks |
| R-09 | Conduct an immediate comprehensive security audit, then establish a fixed monthly audit schedule going forward | IT Manager (acting security lead) | 2 weeks for the initial audit, then monthly thereafter |

## Medium Priority

| Risk ID | Remediation Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-04 | Reset access rights for all employees according to the Least Privilege principle, with clearly defined, limited granting authority per department manager | HR, in collaboration with department managers | 1–3 weeks |
| R-06 | Submit a formal recommendation to senior management to establish a dedicated, full-time cybersecurity manager role (via promotion with backfill, or external hire) | GRC/consulting function raises the recommendation; final decision rests with senior management and HR | 4–6 weeks |
| R-08 | Review and consolidate all outdated procedures to align with the updated (2026) versions | IT team (ownership to transfer to a dedicated security function once established) | 3–4 weeks |

---

## Notes on Sequencing

Several remediation items are interdependent. Establishing a formal risk management process (R-03) is more effective once a dedicated cybersecurity owner is in place (R-06); likewise, strategy updates (R-07) and role documentation (R-05) benefit from the same appointment. Where possible, R-06 should be initiated early, even though it is scored as Medium, since it unblocks faster and more durable resolution of several Critical items.
