# Access Control Policy — TechNova Solutions

**Document Owner:** IT Department
**Version:** 1.0
**Related NCA ECC Control:** 2-2 — Identity and Access Management

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy ensures that access to TechNova Solutions' systems and information is granted only to authorized individuals, and only to the minimum extent necessary to perform their job functions (the principle of Least Privilege), thereby reducing the risk of unauthorized access and protecting sensitive data.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees (whether working on-site or remotely) and contractors who access TechNova Solutions' systems or data. Each user is granted the access required to perform their job function only, with no additional privileges beyond the scope of their role.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Direct Department Manager:** Approves access requests based on the employee's actual job requirements
- **Human Resources (HR):** Creates the user account and grants baseline access upon hiring, in coordination with the IT team
- **IT Team:** Activates additional access following manager approval, and manages accounts technically (creation, modification, deactivation)
- **Employee:** Uses only the access granted to them, reports any access they suspect is unauthorized, and does not share login credentials with any other person

## 4. Policy Statements

1. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) must be enabled on all sensitive systems for every user, with no exceptions
2. A company-approved VPN must be used for any remote access to internal systems; direct connections over the public internet are not permitted
3. HR must notify the IT team of an employee's last working day at least one day in advance, and the employee's account must be fully deactivated on the same day they leave — without delay
4. All user access rights must be reviewed at least every 90 days to confirm alignment with current job responsibilities, with any excess or unused access revoked immediately

## 5. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy (for example, granting the IT team temporarily expanded access while responding to a security incident) must be formally approved by the IT Manager or senior management, with the reason and duration of the exception documented. The exception must be automatically revoked once the need has ended, or within a maximum of 7 days.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations of this policy result in the following actions:

- **Sharing login credentials:** The password is changed immediately (preventive measure), in addition to a formal written warning to the employee involved
- **Attempting to bypass or disable Multi-Factor Authentication:** A formal written warning, escalated to senior management in the event of repeated occurrences — given the severity of this action to the security of all systems
- **Failing to use VPN for remote work:** Remote work privileges are suspended for 3 months, with the employee required to work on-site during this period

Any repeated or serious violation is subject to further disciplinary action under HR policy.

## 7. Review Cycle

This policy is reviewed at least every 6 months by the IT team, or immediately following any major change to the company's technical infrastructure or the emergence of new security threats that require an update.
